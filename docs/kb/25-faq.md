# Frequently Asked Questions

## General

### What is VendShop AI Studio?
AI Studio is a creative platform that helps small businesses create professional images and videos using artificial intelligence. No design skills required — just describe what you want.

### Do I need design experience?
No. You describe what you want in plain language, and AI creates it for you. Style presets help you get professional results without knowing photography or design terminology.

### What languages are supported?
The interface supports Slovak, English, Ukrainian, Czech, and German. You can write prompts in any language — the AI understands all major languages.

### Can I use the images commercially?
Yes. All images generated in AI Studio are yours to use for any commercial purpose — on your website, social media, menus, marketing materials, etc.

## Credits & Billing

### How many free credits do I get?
Free plan includes 15 image credits per month. No video credits on free plan.

### Do unused credits carry over?
Monthly credits do NOT carry over — they reset every 30 days. Bonus credits from credit packs DO carry over indefinitely.

### When do my credits reset?
Every 30 days from when you started your subscription (or from your first credit reset).

### Can I get more credits without upgrading?
Yes. Buy a credit pack: Pack S (€12 — 120 images + 5 videos) or Pack L (€29 — 350 images + 15 videos).

### What happens if I run out of credits mid-month?
You can buy a credit pack for immediate top-up, or wait for your monthly reset. Free tools (Auto Split, Place Products, Quick Filters) continue to work.

### Are there any free tools?
Yes! These tools cost 0 credits: Grok Imagine (image generation), Grok Edit (image editing), Auto Split, Place Products (manual compositing), Quick Filters. AI Blend after Place Products costs 1 credit.

## Technical

### What image quality can I expect?
Quality depends on your tier: Quick (1 credit) is good for drafts, Best (2 credits) is high quality for social media, HD (3 credits) is the highest quality for print and professional use.

### How long does generation take?
Images: 3-15 seconds depending on quality tier. Videos: 30-120 seconds depending on duration. Longer videos take more time.

### Can I generate videos?
Yes, but video credits are required. Free plan has no video credits. Upgrade to Starter (€9/month — 5 video credits) or Pro (€19/month — 15 video credits).

### What's BYOK?
BYOK (Bring Your Own Key) lets you use your own API keys from AI providers (Replicate, xAI, fal.ai, BFL). The BYOK Creator plan (€7/month) gives you unlimited usage with your own keys.

### Why did my generation fail?
Common reasons: prompt too short/long, rate limit exceeded, temporary provider issue. See the Troubleshooting guide for detailed solutions.

### Can I edit generated images?
Yes! Use Improve Photo to enhance, Inpaint to change specific areas, Remove Background to isolate subjects, or Upscale to increase resolution.

### Is there a mobile version?
Studio works in any modern mobile browser. The interface is responsive and touch-friendly. For best results, we recommend a tablet or desktop for detailed editing work.

## VendShop Platform

### What is VendShop?
VendShop (vendshop.shop) is a platform for creating online stores for small businesses. AI Studio is one part of the platform — it provides creative tools for generating marketing content.

### Do I need a VendShop store to use Studio?
No. AI Studio is available to anyone with a VendShop account, even if you don't have an online store on the platform.

### What types of stores does VendShop support?
9 types: physical retail, food & grocery, restaurant, beauty salon, repair service, home services, digital products, education, and health/wellness.
