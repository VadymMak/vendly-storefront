## Credit Packs (One-Time Purchase)

If you run out of monthly credits, you can buy extra:

- **Starter Pack** — €{{pack:starter:price}}: {{pack:starter:images}} image credits + {{pack:starter:videos}} video credits
- **Creator Pack** — €{{pack:creator:price}}: {{pack:creator:images}} image credits + {{pack:creator:videos}} video credits (most popular)
- **Pro Pack** — €{{pack:pro:price}}: {{pack:pro:images}} image credits + {{pack:pro:videos}} video credits

Bonus credits from packs are used first (before monthly credits). Bonus credits never expire and carry over between months.

## What Costs Credits

- Every AI feature costs credits — see the Credits System guide for the full list
- AI Style (the Grok finish in Improve Photo) costs {{cost:ai-style}}
- The basic Improve Photo presets (lighting, color, sharpness) are free
- Reels: a photo reel costs {{cost:reel-image}}, an AI video reel costs {{cost:reel-video}}

## How Credits Work

- Monthly credits reset every 30 days (from your last reset, or from each subscription renewal)
- Unused monthly credits do not roll over
- Bonus credits (from packs) are consumed before monthly credits
- Free plan users with bonus credits can access Best and HD quality tiers

## Free Plan Restrictions

- Limited to Quick (fast) quality tier for image generation
- No video generation without purchasing a credit pack
- If you buy a credit pack, you unlock Best quality tier even on Free plan
- Rate limits are lower (5 generations per hour vs 30-80 on paid plans)

## How to Upgrade

1. Go to Studio → Settings, or open "Plans" from the credit counter
2. Choose your plan
3. Complete payment via Stripe (secure checkout)
4. Credits are available immediately after payment

## How to Cancel

You can cancel your subscription anytime from Settings → Manage subscription. Your plan stays active until the end of the current billing period, then you move to the Free plan.
