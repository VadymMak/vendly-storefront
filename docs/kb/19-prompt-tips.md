# Prompt Writing Tips

## General Rules

1. **Be specific** — More detail = better results
2. **Use natural language** — Write like you're describing a photo to someone
3. **Include context** — Mention lighting, angle, mood, style
4. **Use any language** — Write prompts in English, Slovak, Czech, Ukrainian, German, or any other language

## Structure of a Good Prompt

A good image prompt has these components:
- **Subject**: What is the main focus? ("a freshly baked croissant")
- **Setting**: Where is it? ("on a marble counter in a bakery")
- **Lighting**: What's the light like? ("warm morning light from a window")
- **Style**: What's the visual style? ("editorial food photography, shallow depth of field")
- **Composition**: What angle? ("overhead shot", "close-up", "45-degree angle")

## Examples by Business Type

### Restaurant
❌ "food photo" (too vague)
✅ "professional food photography of a grilled salmon fillet with asparagus and lemon, on a white ceramic plate, rustic wooden table, warm restaurant lighting, editorial style"

### Barbershop
❌ "haircut" (too vague)
✅ "professional barbershop interior, vintage leather chair, warm lighting, brick walls, grooming tools on shelf, cinematic style"

### Beauty Salon
❌ "nails" (too vague)
✅ "close-up of perfectly manicured hands with soft pink gel nails, minimalist white background, soft studio lighting, beauty editorial style"

### Auto Repair
❌ "car" (too vague)
✅ "modern auto repair workshop interior, clean organized tools on wall, lifted car on hydraulic lift, professional lighting, trust-building commercial photography"

### Online Store
❌ "product" (too vague)
✅ "professional e-commerce product photo of a leather handbag, white studio background, soft shadow, clean minimalist style, 45-degree angle"

## Video Prompt Tips

For text-to-video, describe **motion and action**:
- ❌ "coffee shop" (static)
- ✅ "camera slowly pans across a cozy coffee shop, steam rising from a freshly poured latte, warm golden light, customers in soft focus background"

Include timing cues:
- "starts with a close-up of the product, then slowly zooms out to reveal the full scene"
- "camera orbits around the product on a turntable"

## Prompt Length

- **Minimum:** 3 characters
- **Maximum:** 2000 characters for images, 1000 characters for videos
- **Sweet spot:** 50-200 characters for most images

## What to Avoid

- Don't use technical AI jargon (like "4k render, octane, unreal engine") — our models don't need it
- Don't copy prompts from other AI tools — they may use different syntax
- Don't include negative prompts (what NOT to show) — our models work best with positive descriptions
