# Remove Background

## Overview

Instantly remove the background from any image, creating a clean cutout with transparent background. Perfect for product photos, profile pictures, and compositing.

## How to use

1. Upload or select an image in Studio
2. Click "Remove Background" in Quick Tools
3. The AI processes your image and returns a PNG with transparent background
4. Download or use the cutout in Place Products

## Credits

1 image credit per removal.

## Best for

- **Product photos** — Clean product cutouts for e-commerce listings
- **Profile pictures** — Remove distracting backgrounds
- **Compositing** — Prepare objects for Place Products tool
- **Marketing materials** — Isolate subjects for designs

## Tips

- Works best with clear subject/background separation
- Higher contrast between subject and background gives cleaner edges
- The output is always PNG (to support transparency)
- Use the cutout with Place Products to put your product on any background
