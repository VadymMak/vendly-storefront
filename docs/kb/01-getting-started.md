# Getting Started with VendShop AI Studio

## What is AI Studio?

VendShop AI Studio is an AI-powered creative platform built into vendshop.shop. It helps small businesses create professional images and videos without hiring a photographer or designer.

## Who is it for?

AI Studio is designed for small business owners: restaurants, barbershops, beauty salons, repair shops, clinics, online stores, and more. No design skills required — describe what you want in plain language and AI creates it.

## How to access Studio

1. Go to [vendshop.shop/studio](https://vendshop.shop/studio)
2. Sign in with your VendShop account (or create one for free)
3. You'll see the Studio dashboard with all available tools

## First steps

1. **Generate your first image**: Click "Generate Image", describe what you want (e.g., "professional photo of a cozy barbershop interior"), choose a style preset, and click Generate
2. **Try Quick Tools**: Use one-click tools like Remove Background, Upscale HD, or Improve Photo on any image
3. **Check your credits**: Your credit balance is shown in the top bar. Free plan includes 15 image credits per month

## Available tools

| Tool | What it does | Credits |
|------|-------------|---------|
| Generate Image | Create images from text descriptions | 1-3 per image |
| Generate Video | Create videos from text descriptions | 8-25 per video |
| Improve Photo | Enhance lighting, clarity, and detail | 2 credits |
| Remove Background | Isolate subject with clean cutout | 1 credit |
| Upscale HD | Boost resolution up to 4x | 1 credit |
| Animate Video | Turn a still image into a short video | 5 credits |
| Edit & Replace (Inpaint) | Paint over areas to change or remove objects | 2 credits |
| Place Products | Composite cutouts into scenes | Free |
| Auto Split | Automatically segment objects from an image | Free |
| AI Blend | Harmonize edges after compositing | 1 credit |
| Scene Compose | Combine multiple images into one scene | 2 credits |
| Quick Filters | Apply color filters (Vivid, Warm, Cool, B&W, etc.) | Free |

## Languages

Studio interface supports 5 languages: Slovak (SK), English (EN), Ukrainian (UK), Czech (CS), and German (DE). You can write prompts in any language.
