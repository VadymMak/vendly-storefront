# Privacy & Security

## Your Data

- Generated images belong to you
- Your images are stored securely and only accessible to you
- API keys are encrypted with AES encryption before storage
- We never share your data with third parties

## AI Provider Processing

When you generate images or videos, your prompts are sent to AI providers (Replicate, xAI, fal.ai, BFL) for processing. These providers:
- Process your prompt to generate the result
- May temporarily store the prompt during processing
- Do not use your prompts to train their models (per their terms of service)

## GDPR Compliance

VendShop is operated from the EU and complies with GDPR:
- You can export all your data (Account → Export Data)
- You can delete your account and all data (Account → Delete Account)
- We process minimal personal data (email, name, usage stats)
- To prevent abuse of free credits, sign-ups are limited per network: we store only a one-way hash of your IP address (never the IP itself) and delete it after 30 days
- Sign-up is protected by Cloudflare Turnstile (a privacy-friendly bot check)
- Full privacy policy at vendshop.shop/datenschutz

## Payment Security

- All payments processed via Stripe (PCI DSS Level 1 certified)
- We never see or store your card number
- Secure checkout with SSL/TLS encryption

## Stored API Keys

Own API keys are not offered to customers. Keys saved earlier:
- API keys are encrypted before storage
- Keys are never logged or exposed in API responses
- You can delete your keys anytime
- Keys are only used when you request a generation
