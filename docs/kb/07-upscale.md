# Upscale HD

## Overview

Increase the resolution of any image up to 4x using AI upscaling. Makes small or low-resolution images sharp and detailed.

## How to use

1. Upload or select an image in Studio
2. Click "Upscale HD" in Quick Tools
3. The AI enhances the resolution
4. Download the high-resolution result

## Credits

1 image credit per upscale.

## When to use

- Low-resolution images from social media or messaging apps
- Small product photos that need to be used on websites or print
- AI-generated images that need higher resolution for print
- Old photos that need sharpening

## Tips

- Upscaling works best on images that are at least 256×256 pixels
- The AI adds realistic detail — it doesn't just stretch pixels
- For best results, upscale before applying other edits
