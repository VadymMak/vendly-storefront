# Account & Settings

## Registration

1. Go to [vendshop.shop/register](https://vendshop.shop/register)
2. Enter your email and create a password
3. Verify your email
4. You automatically start on the Free plan with 15 image credits

## Studio Settings

Access settings from the gear icon in Studio or go to Studio → Settings.

### API Keys
Add your own provider API keys for BYOK usage. See the BYOK guide for details.

### Subscription Management
- View your current plan
- Upgrade or downgrade
- Manage payment method via Stripe portal
- Cancel subscription

### Credit Balance
- View remaining monthly credits (image + video)
- View bonus credits from packs
- See when monthly credits reset

## Account Actions

### Export Data
You can export all your account data from account settings. This includes your profile, generation history, and stored images.

### Delete Account
You can permanently delete your account from account settings. This removes all your data, images, and cancels any active subscription.
