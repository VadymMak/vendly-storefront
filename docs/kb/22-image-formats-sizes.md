# Image Formats and Sizes

## Output Formats

| Format | File Size | Quality | Transparency | Best for |
|--------|----------|---------|-------------|----------|
| WebP | Smallest | High | Yes | Websites, fast loading |
| PNG | Large | Lossless | Yes | Print, when transparency needed |
| JPEG | Medium | Good | No | Universal compatibility |

Default output is WebP (best web performance).

## Size Presets

### Social Media Sizes
| Preset | Aspect Ratio | Resolution | Platform |
|--------|-------------|------------|----------|
| Instagram | 4:5 | 1080×1350 | Instagram feed |
| Square | 1:1 | 1080×1080 | Profile pics, thumbnails |
| Story/Reel/TikTok | 9:16 | 1080×1920 | Stories, Reels, TikTok |

### Website Sizes
| Preset | Aspect Ratio | Resolution | Use |
|--------|-------------|------------|-----|
| Landscape/Website | 16:9 | 1920×1080 | Website banners, covers |
| Product/E-commerce | 1:1 | 800×800 | Online store listings |

### Additional Sizes

## Video Aspect Ratios

| Ratio | Name | Best for |
|-------|------|----------|
| 16:9 | Landscape | YouTube, websites |
| 1:1 | Square | Instagram feed, Facebook |
| 9:16 | Portrait | TikTok, Reels, Stories |

## Downloading Images

After generation:
1. Click the download button on the result
2. Choose your preferred format (WebP, PNG, or JPEG)
3. The image downloads at the resolution specified by your size preset

For higher resolution, use Upscale HD (1 credit) to boost up to 4x.
