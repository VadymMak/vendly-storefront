# Place Products (Scene Composition)

## Overview

Compose product cutouts into professional scenes. Drag, drop, resize, and rotate your product images onto any background — then use AI Blend to make the edges seamless.

## How to use

1. Open Place Products from Quick Tools
2. Choose or upload a background image
3. Add cutout images (from Remove Background or Auto Split)
4. Drag, resize, and position your products on the scene
5. Click "AI Blend" to harmonize the edges
6. Download the final composition

## Two-Stage Process

### Stage 1 — Manual Composition (Free)
Drag and drop cutouts onto a background. You can:
- Resize objects (drag corners)
- Rotate objects
- Reposition objects
- Delete objects (hover over thumbnail → click delete)
- Layer objects (change order)

This stage is completely free — no credits needed.

### Stage 2 — AI Blend (1 credit)
After positioning your products, click AI Blend. The AI:
- Harmonizes the edges between objects and background
- Matches lighting and shadows
- Creates smooth, natural-looking transitions
- Makes the composition look like a single photo

AI Blend costs 1 image credit.

## Workflow Example

1. Take a photo of your product
2. Use Remove Background to get a clean cutout (1 credit)
3. Generate a beautiful background with Image Generation (1-3 credits)
4. Open Place Products, add background + cutout
5. Position your product perfectly
6. Hit AI Blend for seamless result (1 credit)

Total: 3-5 credits for a professional product scene.

## Tips

- Use Auto Split to quickly separate multiple products from one photo
- Higher quality backgrounds give better AI Blend results
- Position products at natural angles — AI Blend respects perspective
- You can add multiple products to one scene
