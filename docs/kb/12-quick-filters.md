# Quick Filters

## Overview

Apply instant color filters to any image. These are CSS-based filters applied directly in the browser — no AI processing, no credits needed, completely free.

## Available Filters

1. **Original** — No filter (reset)
2. **Vivid** — Enhanced colors and saturation
3. **Warm** — Warm, golden tones
4. **Cool** — Cool, blue tones
5. **B&W** — Black and white
6. **Sepia** — Classic sepia tone
7. **Vintage** — Faded retro look
8. **Dramatic** — High contrast dramatic look
9. **Soft** — Soft, dreamy effect
10. **Fade** — Washed out, film-like

## How to use

1. Select an image in Studio
2. Open the Quick Filters panel
3. Click any filter to preview
4. The filter is applied instantly (no processing time)

## Credits

Free — no credits required. Quick Filters are applied client-side.

## Tips

- Filters can be combined with other edits
- Apply a filter before downloading for social media consistency
- B&W and Sepia work great for professional brand aesthetics
