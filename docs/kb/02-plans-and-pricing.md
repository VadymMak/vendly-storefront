# Plans and Pricing

## Subscription Plans

VendShop AI Studio offers 4 plans:

### Free Plan — €0/month
- 5 image credits per month
- 0 video credits (video requires a paid plan or credit pack)
- Quick (fast) quality tier only
- Access to all tools
- Best for: trying out the platform

### Starter Plan — €9/month
- 100 image credits per month
- 20 video credits per month
- All quality tiers (Quick, Best, HD)
- Up to 50 photo reels or 4 AI video reels per month
- Own API keys (BYOK) supported
- Best for: regular content creation

### Pro Plan — €19/month
- 300 image credits per month
- 60 video credits per month
- All quality tiers (Quick, Best, HD)
- Up to 150 photo reels or 12 AI video reels per month
- Priority queue
- Own API keys (BYOK) supported
- Best for: heavy content production

### BYOK Creator — €7/month
- Unlimited usage with your own API keys (for the providers you added keys for)
- No credit deductions on those tools (you pay providers directly)
- Reels still use credits — they run on VendShop's own AI keys, so buy a credit pack for reels
- All models unlocked
- Priority queue
- Best for: developers and power users who have their own API keys

Note: on Starter and Pro, adding your own API keys does not skip credits — only the BYOK Creator plan does.

## Credit Packs (One-Time Purchase)

If you run out of monthly credits, you can buy extra:

- **Starter Pack** — €5: 50 image credits + 3 video credits
- **Creator Pack** — €10: 150 image credits + 8 video credits (most popular)
- **Pro Pack** — €20: 400 image credits + 20 video credits

Bonus credits from packs are used first (before monthly credits). Bonus credits never expire and carry over between months.

## What Costs Credits

- Every AI feature costs credits — see the Credits System guide for the full list
- AI Style (the Grok finish in Improve Photo) costs 1 image credit
- The basic Improve Photo presets (lighting, color, sharpness) are free
- Reels: a photo reel costs 2 image credits, an AI video reel costs 5 video credits

## How Credits Work

- Monthly credits reset every 30 days (from your last reset, or from each subscription renewal)
- Unused monthly credits do not roll over
- Bonus credits (from packs) are consumed before monthly credits
- Free plan users with bonus credits can access Best and HD quality tiers

## Free Plan Restrictions

- Limited to Quick (fast) quality tier for image generation
- No video generation without purchasing a credit pack
- If you buy a credit pack, you unlock Best quality tier even on Free plan
- Rate limits are lower (5 generations per hour vs 30-80 on paid plans)

## How to Upgrade

1. Go to Studio → Settings, or open "Plans" from the credit counter
2. Choose your plan
3. Complete payment via Stripe (secure checkout)
4. Credits are available immediately after payment

## How to Cancel

You can cancel your subscription anytime from Settings → Manage subscription. Your plan stays active until the end of the current billing period, then you move to the Free plan.
