# Video Generation

## Text-to-Video

Create videos from text descriptions using Grok Video 1.5 by xAI.

### How to use

1. Go to Studio → Generate Video
2. Describe your video scene
3. Choose a style (Product, Food, Beauty, Social, Interior)
4. Select duration
5. Select aspect ratio
6. Click Generate

### Duration and Credits

| Duration | Label | Credits | Estimated Time |
|----------|-------|---------|----------------|
| 5 seconds | Quick | 8 credits | ~30 seconds |
| 10 seconds | Social | 16 credits | ~60 seconds |
| 15 seconds | Premium | 25 credits | ~90 seconds |

### Aspect Ratios

- **16:9** (Landscape) — YouTube, website banners
- **1:1** (Square) — Instagram feed, Facebook
- **9:16** (Portrait) — TikTok, Instagram Reels, Stories

### Video Style Presets

- **Product** — Clean product showcase
- **Food** — Appetizing food scenes
- **Beauty** — Professional beauty content
- **Social** — Optimized for social platforms
- **Interior** — Interior and architectural scenes

### Tips for Better Videos

1. Describe motion: "a camera slowly panning across a table of desserts"
2. Describe atmosphere: "warm golden hour lighting, soft bokeh"
3. Keep prompts under 1000 characters
4. Start with 5-second videos to test your prompts (cheaper)

## Image-to-Video (Animate)

Turn a still image into a short animated video.

### How to use

1. Upload or select an image in Studio
2. Click "Animate Video" in Quick Tools
3. Optionally describe the motion you want
4. Choose camera motion (zoom in, pan left, orbit, etc.)
5. Click Generate

### Camera Motion Options

- Zoom in / Zoom out
- Pan left / Pan right
- Tilt up / Tilt down
- Orbit left / Orbit right
- None (AI decides)

Motion intensity can be set from 0 to 10.

### Motion Presets

- **Cinematic** — Slow, dramatic camera movement
- **Food** — Subtle zoom with steam/liquid motion
- **Product Showcase** — Orbit around the product
- **Portrait** — Gentle parallax effect
- **Parallax** — 3D depth parallax

### Credits

Animate Video costs 5 video credits per generation.

## Requirements

- Video generation requires a paid plan (Starter, Pro, or BYOK Creator) or a credit pack with video credits
- Free plan users cannot generate videos without purchasing credits
